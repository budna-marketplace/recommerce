# A used drill for furniture assembly

Illustrative output, written by the project authors. This is not a measured model result. All product names, listing details, and prices below are fictional.

## Buyer request

> I need a drill for assembling flat-pack furniture. My maximum total is 1,000 SEK including delivery. I already have the correct screwdriver bits. Is this worth pursuing, and what should I check?

## Supplied evidence

- **Listing A, seller claim:** Northbank D18 drill, one B18 battery, and C18 charger for 795.00 SEK. “Works normally.” Photos supplied by the seller show those three model labels. These labels have not been checked in person.
- **Document B, fictional model documentation:** D18 supports screwdriving, has adjustable torque, and supports the B18 battery. C18 is the specified charger for B18. This describes the models, not the condition of these particular items.
- **Quote C, seller claim:** Delivery to the buyer's destination is 95.00 SEK, with no other mandatory charges. The seller explicitly says the item is available as of 2026-10-28 at 09:00 UTC; it is not reserved.
- **Observation D, buyer:** The buyer already has suitable bits, but has not inspected the offered equipment.

## Example decision card

**Worth pursuing, subject to condition checks.** The documented D18 functions fit your stated furniture-assembly task. The seller describes the required drill, battery, and charger, and you already have the bits. Their exact model combination is supported by Document B.

**Quoted total: 890.00 SEK** — 795.00 + 95.00 delivery. That leaves 110.00 SEK within your inclusive maximum. This uses the seller's quoted terms; it is not an independent checkout quote.

**Before committing:** ask about known faults, repairs, or abnormal battery behavior. “Works normally” is a seller claim, not a battery-capacity test. Confirm current availability and the quoted delivery terms if time has passed.

**If viewing in person:** compare the actual model labels and included equipment with the listing, and look for visible damage to the tool, battery, charger, cable, and plug. Do not power or charge visibly damaged equipment. If operation will be demonstrated, use the exact model's manual and a competent operator; leave it unverified if that cannot be done safely.

**Still unverified:** actual condition, remaining battery capacity, and latent faults. A brief demonstration cannot establish those fully.

**Next step:** get the fault history and decide whether a safe inspection or suitable evidence of operation is possible before committing.
