# Cordless drills: decision and inspection guidance

Review draft, source review dated 2026-10-28. Covers ordinary DIY drill purchases; it is not a repair manual, exhaustive defect checklist, or substitute for the exact product's instructions. The sources below support the safety limits. Model suitability and compatibility still require documentation for the item being assessed.

## Before recommending the listing

Resolve the intended job only as far as needed: material, drilling or driving, and any relevant size or workload. A familiar brand name does not establish that this particular model is suitable. Do not treat a drill, impact driver, and rotary hammer as interchangeable because the listing uses the same search terms.

Identify the exact tool model, its battery/charging arrangement, and included accessories. Some related cordless screwdrivers have integrated batteries; use their exact instructions rather than inventing a requirement for a removable pack or another tool's charger. Read the description as well as the title. Photographs of a retail box or promotional kit do not establish that its pictured contents are supplied. Ask only for the specific inclusion or label that matters.

For a buyer who owns batteries or a charger, check the actual compatibility statements for those exact models. Physical fit, nominal voltage, and a shared brand are insufficient evidence of an approved combination. Use a manufacturer compatibility table or manual; if inaccessible, describe the match as unverified. Manufacturer instructions specify suitable battery and charger combinations and prohibit damaged or modified packs. [Bosch drill safety instructions, battery-tool use and care](https://ocsmedia.boschtools.com/binary/manualsmedia/o273782v2_1605A0032C_GSB18V65_OSI_20250115.pdf).

Count the components needed for the stated job, including suitable bits when the buyer lacks them. Separate required extras from optional conveniences such as a second battery or case. A bare tool can be a complete solution for someone who already owns compatible essentials.

For imported chargers, the exact regional model, input rating, and suitability for the buyer's supply need verification. Do not recommend a plug adapter as proof of electrical compatibility.

## Choose checks from the actual evidence

Use this menu selectively. Do not turn every purchase into a long questionnaire.

| Listing uncertainty | Useful check | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| “Complete” with ambiguous contents | Ask which tool, batteries, charger, and relevant bits are included; request readable model labels if needed. | That every component works. |
| Condition described only as “works” | Ask about known faults, repairs, modifications, battery behavior, and whether a safe demonstration is possible. | That an unsupported seller assurance is independently verified. |
| Photos omit important areas | At collection, visually compare the actual tool, labels, battery, charger, cable, and plug with the listing. Look for visible damage or modification without opening anything. | Internal condition or authenticity. |
| A specific intermittent fault is disclosed | Name the fault as a purchase risk. Ask for its history and qualified assessment; do not invent an aggressive test to provoke it. | That failing to reproduce the fault makes it resolved. |
| A demonstration appears normal | Credit what it demonstrates for the intended job; record the conditions. Do not require a capacity test for light use without a relevant symptom or endurance requirement. | Remaining battery capacity, sustained performance, or absence of latent faults. |
| Availability is unclear | Ask for explicit current availability before travelling or committing. | That a recently fetched listing is still available. |

Check the exact manual before suggesting any powered demonstration. It should be performed only by someone competent, with the manufacturer's prescribed setup and protective measures; do not give improvised operating instructions in an inspection card. Where a safe demonstration cannot be arranged, leave operation unverified or suggest qualified inspection. [Bosch drill operating and safety instructions](https://ocsmedia.boschtools.com/binary/manualsmedia/o273782v2_1605A0032C_GSB18V65_OSI_20250115.pdf).

Separate superficial wear from suspected safety damage: a scratched label alone does not establish a faulty tool, while a swollen/split battery, exposed wiring, or damaged plug warrants stopping powered testing. Do not recommend opening a pack or charger, probing terminals, reviving a depleted pack, or bypassing protection. Refer suspected safety damage to a qualified service provider. [Bosch GAL18V-40 charger safety rules, pages 2–3](https://www.bosch-professional.com/binary/manualsmedia/o423073v21_1605A0019V_GAL18V-40_20230124.pdf).

## Decide without overclaiming

An affordable item that lacks a required battery or charger is not yet an affordable working setup. Price the missing approved components from evidence, or leave the total unresolved. Do not invent replacement prices or diagnose a battery as the cause of a fault from a seller's description alone.

Reject an established job mismatch or an all-in cost above the buyer's limit. Recommend keeping an existing usable tool or obtaining a qualified repair assessment when the supplied facts make that more suitable. Avoid assuming that buying something is the desired outcome.

For a promising item, choose the single most consequential unresolved fact as the next step. A complete, documented kit should receive a direct conditional recommendation rather than repeated questions about already established inclusions.
